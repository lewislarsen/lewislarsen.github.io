### Welcome
This is where my future blog entries will go! Don't expect too much, however if I find something interesting I'll be putting it here. :)

### Why now?

I've never really been one to jot down my thoughts, a few days ago I was laying in bed and thinking about what I could work on, and I realized I've never really had a blog to express and share my opinions and feelings.

I expect this blog to primarily be sharing little tips or reviews I've had within the PHP and Laravel ecosystems, but I do intend on sharing real-life and personal challenges if they are appropriate.

### Plans and Goals for 2025

Even though this is normally done at the beginning of the year, considering it's the beginning of my blog journey I thought it might be appropriate nonetheless. 

Starting a few weeks ago, I'm focusing more on my physical health. I've struggled with an eating disorder since my mid-teens, and it occasionally comes back with a vengeance. I've been too complacent, and I've been suffering as a consequence.

I want to work more on my side projects, and improve [Vanguard](https://larsens.dev/projects/vanguard/). Currently, the project is fairly unknown but raising its publicity and getting more contributors would be amazing, and nerve-wrecking at the same time.

To summarize:

- Work on improving my health, physical and mental
- Continued updates on side projects
- Expand and grow my skill as a dev
