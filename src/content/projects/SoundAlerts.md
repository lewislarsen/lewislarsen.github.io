# Sound Alerts Expanded

I am a long term RuneScape player and have used the RuneLite client for many years. I wanted to add some additional sound alerts to the client, so I created this plugin.

The plugin is supposed to be supplementary to existing sound alert plugins available on the RuneLite Plugin Hub. It adds sound alerts to various activities in the game that aren't covered by other plugins, such as:

- **Farming** - Alerts for when you're at a herb patch without a max cape or farming cape equipped. (The cape's perk allows a chance for additional herbs to be harvested, and I kept forgetting to equip it.)
